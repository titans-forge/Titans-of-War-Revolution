Generated by the built-in image tool, using the existing inspected march atlas as a material/uniform reference. Original: `[private provenance path omitted]`.

The unchanged source contains a clothed torso with head/arms/musket, a thigh, a calf and a foot. The renderer keys magenta, crops measured frames and creates padded textures. Calf and ankle crops intentionally omit pale overlap cuffs; the separate leg pieces overlap at measured joints. No manual raster editing or local image-model runner was used.

Near and far legs share the authored material but have independent hip/knee/ankle coordinates. Two-bone geometry provides alternating stance and passing phases, with the near leg consistently drawn over the far leg. This avoids relying on generated pose sheets to infer leg identity. The torso retains its full painted detail. Travel distance drives gait; horizontal stance-foot translation compensates for body movement, adjusted for figure height and formation scale. Vertical/diagonal foot planting remains approximate because the game has two facing views.

The accepted rig is enabled only for infantry profile 0. Other uniforms and stationary, firing, reloading, close-combat and backward-fallback states retain the existing art. A missing rig texture also retains the existing image. This is illustrative game art, not a verified reconstruction of a particular regiment.
