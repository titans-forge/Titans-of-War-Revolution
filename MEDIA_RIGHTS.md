Any later image, font, music track, recording, or model-produced asset must be added here with its creator or institution, source or generation system, license or terms basis, attribution, and review status before entering a public build. Unresolved media stays outside `public/` and release bundles.

Attribution: Titans Forge / OpenAI; use basis: project-generated output under the applicable OpenAI terms, not a public-domain claim. Visually reviewed locally; no publication this pass. Bunker Hill reuses the existing reviewed background.
