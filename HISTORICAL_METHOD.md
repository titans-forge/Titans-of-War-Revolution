# Historical Method

Titans of War: Revolution follows the Titans Forge Social Science Rubric.

- Avoid presentism. Explain actors through the language, institutions, interests, and choices available in their own period.
- Prefer primary sources and strong historical scholarship. Keep citations accessible without turning the decision surface into a research paper.
- Show power, incentives, logistics, political authority, finance, sectional interests, and military constraints.
- Distinguish documented fact, reasonable inference, and alternate-history speculation.
- Preserve complexity. Patriots, Loyalists, neutrals, Native nations, enslaved and free Black people, soldiers, merchants, creditors, states, and foreign powers did not possess one interest or one motive.
- Do not invent quotations for historical people.

Every scenario should carry a source note, a factual outcome, and a clear statement of what the player is allowed to change.
